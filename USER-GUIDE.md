# How to Use the Greenville Disc Golf Club Website 🥏

This guide explains **how to do everything** on the club website, using very simple words. Each thing is a short list of baby steps: do step 1, then step 2, then you're done. 🎉

There are four parts:

1. **[For Everybody](#part-1-for-everybody-no-login-needed)** — things anyone can do, no login needed.
2. **[For Members](#part-2-for-members-you-need-a-login)** — things you do after you log in.
3. **[Live Scoring](#part-3-live-scoring-keeping-score-during-a-round)** — keeping score while you play.
4. **[For Admins](#part-4-for-admins-the-control-room)** — the special control room for club helpers.

> **Tip:** On a phone, look for the little **menu button** (three lines ☰) in the top corner to find the pages.

---

## Part 1: For Everybody (no login needed)

### 🏠 Look at the website
1. Open your web browser (like Chrome or Safari).
2. Go to **greenvillediscgolf.com**.
3. That's it — you're on the home page! Scroll down with your finger or mouse to see more.

### 📅 See upcoming tournaments and events
1. On the home page, scroll down to the tournaments part, **or** tap **Club Events** in the menu.
2. You'll see a list of games coming up. Newest and soonest are near the top.
3. Tap any event to see more about it: where it is, which course or courses and layouts it uses, who signed up, and the scores if it already happened.

### ⛳ Look at the disc golf courses
1. On the home page, scroll to the **courses** part.
2. Tap a course card (the little picture box with a course name).
3. A pop-up window opens. It has buttons:
   - **View on UDisc** — see the course in the UDisc app/website.
   - **Get Directions** — open a map to drive there.
   - **Course Preview** — watch a video of the course (if there is one).
4. To close the pop-up, tap the **Close** button or tap outside the box. You can also press the **Esc** key on a keyboard.

### 🏆 See who's winning (Ryder Cup & standings)
1. Tap **Ryder Cup** in the menu.
2. You'll see two teams: **Red** and **Blue**. The page shows how many points each team has and who won each week.
3. For league standings, go to **Club Events** and look for **Leagues & Standings**.

### 👀 Watch a game happen live
1. When a game is being played right now, its event page shows a **live** banner.
2. Tap **View live leaderboard**.
3. The scores update all by themselves while people play — you don't have to refresh! It's like watching a scoreboard at a real game.

### 📝 Read the blog
1. Tap **Blog** in the menu.
2. Read the club news and stories.

### 📜 See old results
1. Tap **Previous Results** (the archive page).
2. Pick a past event to see who won and all the scores.

### 🤖 Talk to Crotts, the helper robot
1. On most pages, look in the **bottom-left corner** for a round button with a face on it. That's **Crotts**.
2. Tap it. A little chat window opens.
3. Type a question like "When is the next tournament?" and press send.
4. Crotts knows about the club's events and courses and will try to help. He's like a friendly helper who knows a lot about the club.

### 💚 Give money (donate)
1. On the home page, tap a **Donate** button.
2. It opens **PayPal**. Type how much you want to give and finish in PayPal.

### 🙋 Join the club
1. On the home page, tap **Join the Club** or **Get Started Today**.
2. Follow the steps to become a member. Once you're a member, a club helper gives you a login (see Part 2).

## Part 2: For Members (you need a login)

Being a **member** is like having a key to extra rooms. A club helper (admin) gives you your key.

### 🔑 Get your login
- A club helper makes an account for you and gives you a **secret 4-digit PIN** (like `1234`).
- Your **username** is either your **PDGA number** (your disc golf player number) **or** your **UDisc name**.
- You cannot make your own account — a helper has to make it for you. This keeps the club safe.

### 🚪 Log in the very first time (and pick your own PIN)
1. Tap **Members** in the menu.
2. Type your **PDGA number or UDisc name** in the first box.
3. Type the **PIN the helper gave you** in the second box.
4. Tap **Sign in**.
5. Because it's your first time, the site asks you to **pick a new secret PIN** that only you know. Type it twice and save it. 🔒
6. Now you're in! Don't tell anyone your new PIN.

**If you type the wrong PIN too many times,** the door locks for a little while to keep bad guys out. Just wait a few minutes and try again.

### 👆 Set up a passkey (log in with your face or fingerprint)
A passkey means you don't have to type your PIN every time — you can use your fingerprint or face, like unlocking your phone.
1. Log in the normal way first.
2. Look for **Add a passkey** and tap it.
3. Your phone or computer asks for your fingerprint/face. Say yes.
4. Next time, tap **Log in with a passkey** and use your fingerprint/face. Easy!

### 📊 Your dashboard (the Overview tab)
After you log in on the **Members** page, you see your **dashboard**. It has tabs across the top:
- **📊 Overview** — your player info, ratings, and any live games.
- **📝 Events** — sign up for games.
- **💬 Board** — the members' message board.
- **🪧 Tee Signs** — course sign pictures.
- **🥏 Club** — club info.

Tap a tab to switch rooms.

### ⭐ See your rating and stats
1. On the **Overview** tab, look for your **rating** (a number that says how good you're playing) and your **player stats**.
2. Scroll to **GVDG Event History** to see games you played and how you did.

### 🧑 Fix your profile (PDGA number, UDisc name, photo)
1. Find **Edit profile**.
2. Add or fix your **PDGA number** and **UDisc name**.
3. Add a **photo** (pick one from your device). It shows up as your little round picture.
4. Save. If someone else already owns that PDGA number, the site won't let you take it (that's on purpose).

### ✍️ Sign up for a game (register)
1. Go to the **Events** tab (or the "Register for Events" part).
2. Find the game you want. Tap **Register**.
3. Pick your **division** (your skill group, like MA1 or MA40).
4. If you want, add extras: **CTP** (closest-to-the-pin contest) or **ace pot** (a money pot for hole-in-ones).
5. Tap to finish. Now you're signed up! To un-sign-up, tap **Withdraw**.

### 💳 Pay for a game
There are a few ways, and the club picks which one is on:
- **PayPal:** if you owe money, PayPal buttons appear. Tap and pay in PayPal.
- **Store credit (wallet):** if you have club credit, you can use that.
- **Pay at the event:** sometimes it just says "pay at the event" — bring money on game day.

### ✅ Check in on game day
1. When you get to the game, open the event.
2. Tap **Check in**. This tells the helpers you're here and ready to play.

### 💬 Use the message board
1. Go to the **Board** tab.
2. To write something, type in the box and tap **Post**. Everyone in the club can read it.
3. To answer someone, tap **Reply** under their message and type.
4. You can **Delete** your own messages.
- Only members can read and write here — it's a members-only clubhouse wall.

### 🪧 Send a tee-sign picture
A "tee sign" is the sign at each hole that shows the hole number and how far it is.
1. Go to the **Tee Signs** tab.
2. Take or pick a **photo** of a tee sign and upload it.
3. The computer reads the sign and guesses the hole info. A helper checks it and says yes. Thanks for helping the club! 🙌

### 📖 Find another member (directory)
1. Go to the member directory part.
2. Type a name in the **search** box, or tap a filter button.
3. Tap a player to see their info card.

### 🛒 Buy things in the Pro Shop
1. Tap **Pro Shop** in the menu.
2. Look at the items (discs, shirts, and more).
3. Tap what you want and buy it with **PayPal** or your **store credit**.

### 🎟️ Your wallet (store credit)
- Your **wallet** is like a piggy bank of club credit. Helpers can add credit to it (for example, if you win a prize).
- When you buy something, you can spend your credit instead of real money.
- The wallet only ever adds up what really happened, so it can never go below zero.

### 🚪 Log out
1. Tap **Log out** in the menu or top corner.
2. You're signed out. Good idea if you're on a shared computer.

## Part 3: Live Scoring (keeping score during a round)

The **score app** (tap **Live Scoring**, or open a round link someone shares) lets you keep score while you play, like a smart scorecard.

### 🟢 Start or join a scorecard
- **Join with a link:** someone shares a round link or a short **code**. Open the link, or type the code, and you're on the card.
- **Casual round:** you and friends can start your own card to play for fun.
- **Event round:** for club games, a helper starts it, and players get added from who signed up.

### 🔢 Keep score, hole by hole
1. You see one hole at a time with a big number.
2. Tap **−** to lower the score or **+** to raise it for each player.
3. Tap the arrow **›** to go to the next hole, or **‹** to go back.
- **Important:** everyone on the card keeps their **own** score. If two people scoring the same card don't agree on a number, the app leaves that hole blank until they match. This keeps scores honest. 🤝

### 🌦️ Check round weather
- During a live round, the scorecard can show the current course weather.
- Wind starts as north-up. On phones that support device orientation, the wind arrow can switch to phone-relative so it points correctly compared with the direction your phone is facing.
- If the phone or browser does not allow that sensor, the weather still works with the normal wind direction.

### 👯 Doubles, teams, and match-play
- **Doubles:** two players share one score. Set up the pairs in **Manage** before you start.
- **Match-play (Red vs Blue):** the app colors each hole by which team won that hole — **red**, **blue**, or **yellow** for a tie. It's like coloring in who won each little battle.

### 🏆 See the leaderboard
1. Tap **View live leaderboard** (or the trophy button 🏆).
2. A sheet slides up showing everyone's scores in order.
3. It updates by itself as people play.

### ➕ Add a guest or fix the card
1. Tap **Manage** or **+ Player**.
2. You can **add a guest** (someone without an account) or **remove** someone who left.
3. Tap **Share** to send the card link to a friend.

### 🏁 Finish the round
1. When everyone is done, open the leaderboard sheet.
2. Tap **Finish round**.
3. The app only lets you finish if all the scores match up. If they don't, it tells you which hole to fix first.
4. When it's finished, the scores are saved forever and show up in the results and everyone's history. 🎉

## Part 4: For Admins (the control room)

Admins are club helpers with extra powers. The **Admin** page (`admin.html`) is the control room. Regular members can't get in — only admins.

### 🔐 Get into the control room
1. Log in as a member first (Part 2). Your account must be marked as an **admin** by another admin.
2. Tap **Admin** in the menu (you only see this if you're an admin).
3. The control room opens with **tabs** across the top. Each tab is a different job. Here's what each one does:

### 📋 The tabs, explained simply

- **Events** — the list of all games. Tap one to edit its name, date, place, and settings.
- **New Event** — make a brand-new game. Fill in the name, date, and the course/layout rows, then save. Some tournaments use one course; others use several courses or several layouts.
- **Import** — instead of typing a game by hand, **grab** games from other websites (Disc Golf Scene), a spreadsheet (CSV), or UDisc. Paste the link and import.
- **Registration** — set up sign-ups for a game: turn sign-ups **on/off**, set the **entry fee**, make **divisions** (skill groups), add **CTP** contests and **ace pots**, **assign** players to starting holes or teams (random or by hand), see who's signed up, **check people in**, and mark them **paid**.
- **Live Scoring** — as an admin you can **start** a game's scoring, **watch** the live scorecard and leaderboard, and **finish** the round. Admins can fix scores when players can't agree.
- **Courses** — add or edit disc golf **courses** (name, location).
- **Layouts** — set up the **holes** for a course: how many, the par, and the distance. There's a **SAFARI builder** that helps you build custom hole layouts and even guesses distances from map points.
- **Tee Signs** — members send tee-sign photos; here you **look at them and say yes or no** to the info the computer guessed.
- **Leagues** — make a **league** (a season of games) and see the **standings** as games finish.
- **Fundraisers** — make a **fundraiser** with a goal and a progress bar so people can chip in.
- **Meetings** — write down **meeting minutes** (notes) and action items so the club remembers what was decided.
- **Pro Shop** — add and edit the **items** for sale (name, price, picture).
- **Orders** — see everything people **bought** in the pro shop, so you can hand out the items.
- **Wallets** — give a member **store credit** (for example, a prize). Type who and how much.
- **Members** — the people list. Here you can:
   - **Make a new member** — type their name and PDGA/UDisc; the site gives you a **one-time temporary PIN** to hand to them.
   - **Reissue PIN** — if someone forgot their PIN, make a new temporary one.
   - **Make admin / Remove admin** — give a member the control-room key, or take it away. (See the next part.)
- **Data archive** — **download** the club's data to keep a copy.

### 👑 Make someone an admin (or take it away)
This is how you give another person control-room powers, or remove them.
1. Go to the **Members** tab.
2. Find the person in the list.
3. To give powers: tap **Make admin**. Say **yes** to the "are you sure?" question. Now they have a little **admin** label.
4. To remove powers: tap **Remove admin** and say yes.
5. **Safety rule:** the site will **not** let you remove the **last** admin. If you're the only admin, the **Remove admin** button is greyed out — this stops the club from getting locked out with no helpers. There always has to be at least one admin. 🛡️
6. If you remove your **own** admin powers, the site warns you first, because you'll lose the control room right away.

The change happens immediately: a new admin can open the control room right away, and someone who lost powers can't get in anymore.

## Quick help

- **I forgot my PIN.** Ask a club admin — they can give you a new temporary PIN from the **Members** tab. Then you pick a new one at login.
- **I can't sign in and it says locked.** You typed the wrong PIN too many times. Wait a few minutes and try again.
- **A button does nothing.** Some features only turn on when the club sets them up (like PayPal payments). If it says "pay at the event," that's on purpose.
- **The page looks old / didn't change.** Try refreshing. Sometimes the browser keeps an old copy for a little while.
